# Two-day family trip to Kraków

**Budget:** up to EUR 300  
**Planning date:** 27 September 2026  
**Working exchange rate:** EUR 1 = PLN 4.25  
**Maximum in local currency:** PLN 1,275

## Assumptions

- Family of four: two adults and two children aged 7-15.
- The family stays in or near the Old Town, so most central sights are reached
  on foot.
- Accommodation and travel to and from Kraków, including airport transfers,
  are **not included**. The budget covers two sightseeing days only.
- Breakfast is bought from a bakery or supermarket, lunch is at a milk bar or
  similarly inexpensive restaurant, and dinner is at a casual family
  restaurant. Alcohol and souvenir shopping are excluded.
- Reduced public-transport and attraction prices depend on proof of age or
  eligibility. Carry identification and check the rules before buying.
- Prices are planning allowances, not guaranteed quotes. Recheck admission
  prices, opening days, and timed-entry availability before the trip.

## Day 1: Old Town, Planty and medieval Kraków

Most of this day is walkable, with the transport pass available for tired
children or poor weather.

| Time | Plan | Practical notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00-09:00 | Bakery or supermarket breakfast | Buy pastries, fruit, yogurt and drinks rather than using a hotel buffet. |
| 09:00-10:30 | Walk the Planty, Barbican exterior and St. Florian's Gate | Free, outdoors and easy to shorten. Start the 48-hour transport tickets around 09:00. |
| 10:30-12:00 | Main Market Square and Cloth Hall | Free. Watch the hourly bugle call from St. Mary's Tower and let the children explore the square. |
| 12:00-13:00 | Milk-bar lunch | Choose a place a few streets off the square for better value. |
| 13:15-14:45 | Rynek Underground | The main paid activity. Reserve a family slot in advance; the multimedia archaeology displays work well for school-age children. |
| 15:00-16:30 | Collegium Maius courtyard and Old Town scavenger walk | Courtyard and walking activity are free. Look for gargoyles, coats of arms and the smallest details on old façades. |
| 16:30-17:00 | Snack break | Use a bakery or one shared dessert stop. |
| 17:00-18:30 | Planty playground/rest or return to accommodation | Keeps the schedule manageable for children. |
| 18:30-20:00 | Casual dinner outside the Market Square frontage | Check the menu before sitting down and avoid restaurants with tourist-menu pricing. |

## Day 2: Wawel, the dragon and Kraków Zoo

This combines a free landmark morning with a paid afternoon activity. Kraków
Zoo is in Las Wolski and involves slopes and walking, so use comfortable shoes
and take a stroller for younger children.

| Time | Plan | Practical notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00-09:00 | Bakery or supermarket breakfast | Pack refillable water bottles and a small snack for the zoo. |
| 09:00-11:15 | Wawel Hill courtyards and viewpoints | Walking around the hill and courtyards is free. Skip paid castle interiors to protect both time and budget. |
| 11:15-11:45 | Wawel Dragon and Vistula riverbank | Free. The fire-breathing dragon is the child-focused highlight. The seasonal Dragon's Den is not included. |
| 12:00-13:00 | Early milk-bar lunch | Eat before travelling to the zoo; zoo food is usually less flexible and more expensive. |
| 13:00-14:00 | Travel to Kraków Zoo | Use city transport to the Cracovia/Błonia area and bus 134 for the final leg. Confirm the current route in the official journey planner. |
| 14:00-17:00 | Kraków Zoo | Allow about three hours. Buy the family-equivalent combination of two adult and two reduced tickets; the budget includes a price cushion. |
| 17:00-18:00 | Return to the centre | The 48-hour tickets remain valid if activated at about 09:00 on Day 1. |
| 18:30-20:00 | Casual dinner | Use the remaining food allowance at a neighbourhood restaurant. |

The PLN 190 contingency is deliberately substantial. It can absorb modest
price changes, a child who is not eligible for a reduced transport fare, an
extra tram ride, medicine, or a more expensive meal. Do not treat it as planned
spending.

## Booking and money checklist

1. Reserve the Rynek Underground timed entry before the trip and confirm that
   the selected family ticket covers this exact family composition.
2. Check the zoo's opening hours and last entry for the travel date. In severe
   rain, replace the zoo with an indoor attraction whose total family admission
   stays at or below PLN 160.
3. Price two adult and two reduced 48-hour Zone I tickets before purchase. The
   transport allowance assumes a combined cost of about PLN 105. If reduced
   fares are unavailable, use the contingency rather than exceeding the cap.
4. Activate all time-based tickets immediately before the first ride, and
   retain each validated ticket for inspection.
5. Keep at least PLN 100 of the contingency unspent until the final dinner.
6. Use card payments in PLN and decline dynamic currency conversion when a
   terminal offers to charge in euros.

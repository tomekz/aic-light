# Four-day family trip to Kraków

**Budget:** up to EUR 300  
**Planning date:** 27 September 2026  
**Working exchange rate:** EUR 1 = PLN 4.25  
**Maximum in local currency:** PLN 1,275

## Assumptions

- Family of four: two adults and two children aged 7-15.
- The family stays in or near the Old Town in accommodation with a refrigerator
  and basic food-preparation space.
- Accommodation and travel to and from Kraków, including airport transfers,
  are **not included**. The budget covers four full sightseeing days only.
- To make four days realistic within EUR 300, breakfast and packed lunch come
  from a supermarket, snacks are carried, and one inexpensive hot dinner is
  bought daily. Alcohol and souvenirs are excluded.
- Most central journeys are on foot. The transport allowance covers a limited
  number of pay-as-you-go Zone I rides rather than unlimited passes.
- Reduced public-transport and attraction prices depend on proof of age or
  eligibility. Carry identification and check the rules before buying.
- Prices are conservative planning allowances, not guaranteed quotes. Recheck
  admissions, opening days, timed-entry availability, and fares before travel.

The PLN 130 contingency can absorb modest fare or admission changes, medicine,
an extra ride, or one meal running over budget. It is not planned spending. If
reduced transport fares are unavailable, recalculate all rides before buying
and remove a paid attraction if the new total would exceed PLN 1,275.

## Booking and money checklist

1. Reserve Rynek Underground and confirm that the selected family ticket
   covers two adults and two children of the stated ages.
2. Check the Polish Aviation Museum's opening days and current family price.
   If the family admission exceeds PLN 100, use contingency only if the total
   remains below the cap.
3. Check the price of the exact Zone I tickets needed for each trip. The
   PLN 105 allowance assumes reduced child fares and limited rides, not a
   four-day pass.
4. Validate tickets immediately when required and retain them for inspection.
5. Buy four days of breakfast, picnic, and snack supplies from a supermarket
   early in the trip; track the PLN 800 food cap daily.
6. Keep at least PLN 80 of contingency unspent until Day 4.
7. Pay by card in PLN and decline dynamic currency conversion if a terminal
   offers to charge in euros.
